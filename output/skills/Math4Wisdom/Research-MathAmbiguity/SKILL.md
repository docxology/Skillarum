> Mathematics Unfolds Through Ambiguity
>
> Math develops through ambiguity. Through parallel concepts, through equations. For example, the determinant of a matrix is the product of its eigenvalues. This is because it is the last term of the characteristic equation Det(A-lambda I)=0. Through such equations, and the ambiguity they manage, math unfolds.
>
> Variables are the source of ambiguity because they relate two levels of the foursome. Collect examples of variables and ambiguity in the mathematical concepts.
>
> Readings
>
> Bill Byers. The Ambiguity of Mathematics.
