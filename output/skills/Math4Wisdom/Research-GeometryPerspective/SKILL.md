> Investigation: Relate perspective with regularity of choice
>
> Bundles (of fibers, vectors, etc.) are the machinery needed for perspectives. They let us define something like a function that takes a value at each point of a manifold, but in a different copy of the relevant vector space. In this sense, they output their own point. And so that point is also relevant as a "zero", for example, for projective geometry.
>
> Note that the three-cycle is expressed by long exact sequences of bundles. Understand this!
