## 1. Blind reconstruction
Written before opening `SKILL.md`, `mathematics.md` or `interpretations.md`.

- **Carrier.** The cube, with 8 divisions as vertices, 12 circumstances as edges and 6 conceptions as faces. S1 and S6 supply this. The 24 is the number of elements in each of the three pairwise incidence relations.
- **Language pairing.** Each language is one of the three incidence relations:
  - verbalization is vertex–edge (8↔12);
  - argumentation is edge–face (12↔6);
  - narration is face–vertex (6↔8).
  Both the S2 table and S6 lead to this.
- **Persons.** Use the two inscribed tetrahedra (the parity classes) as the four persons:
  - Choosing one tetrahedron to carry the labels gives faces ↔ the 6 unordered pairs. Each face holds one edge of each tetrahedron.
  - Each cube edge joins one A-vertex to one B-vertex, so edges ↔ 4×3 = 12 ordered pairs of distinct persons. This matches S2's "four conceptions yields 12 circumstances".
  - Vertices are persons plus their antipodes, 4×2 = 8.
  - Axes ↔ the 3 pairings.
- **Caveat I would have stated blind.** All 24 rotations permute the four persons (S4 acting on the diagonals). So the cube cannot distinguish God, I, You and Other, and S3's insistence on that needs extra input. I reached this by argument and did not check it at the time.

## 2. Verification
- The script reports M1–M18 all ok.
- **M15.** (computed)
  - T is orthogonal, det = +1, and T³ = I (not −I).
  - It sends the 8_v weights to the odd half-spin set, the even half-spin set to 8_v, and the odd set to the even set. This is a single 3-cycle on the three sets.
  - It also maps all 24 D4 roots (±e_i±e_j) to roots, so it is an automorphism of the root system.
  - It is outside W(D4), because W(D4) fixes each of the three sets and T moves them. M15 holds.
- **M17.** (computed plus argument)
  - I ran an independent search over 11,962 directions: a half-integer grid on [−2,2]⁴, integer directions on [−4,4]⁴, and 3000 Gaussian reals. There were 0 counterexamples. The sorted-multiplicity comparison used the correct form (1,1,6), (4,4), (1,1,3,3).
  - The proof is complete for all real c:
    - T cycles the sets and ⟨Tw,Tc⟩ = ⟨w,c⟩, so some set may be taken to be 8_v.
    - The multiset of ±c_i is negation-symmetric, so a multiplicity-6 value must be 0. That leaves c ∝ e_k.
    - Then the half-spin sets both split 4+4.
  - Unstated step, in X-forge-b-5: a general one-parameter subgroup of Spin(8) is conjugate into the maximal torus, and conjugation preserves graded dimensions. The reduction to c ∈ ℝ⁴ relies on this.
- **M10.** (computed) Vertex–edge, vertex–face and edge–face orbits under the 24 rotations each have 24 elements. The action is simply transitive on all three.
- **M7 and M5/M8/M9 as equivariant statements.** (computed) These are bijections, but they are not S4-equivariant.
  - Only the 12 rotations in A4 act naturally on the pair labels. The 12 odd rotations swap tetrahedra A and B and act as (a,c)→(πc,πa) on edges. Faces go {X,Y}→complement({πX,πY}).
  - In the labelling of M5/M7, the geometric stabilizer of the edge between A-vertex 0 and the antipode of vertex 3 acts as the transposition (0 3). The natural stabilizer of the ordered pair (0,3) would be (1 2).
  - The geometric stabilizer of the face containing A-edge {0,1} is the cyclic Z4 generated by a 4-cycle. The natural stabilizer of {0,1} is ⟨(01),(23)⟩ ≅ V4, which is not cyclic.
  - So "faces = unordered pairs" and "a conception is fixed by a 4-cycle" (I3/M9) cannot both hold as S4-set statements: S4/Z4 and S4/V4 are non-isomorphic. For edges the two S4-sets are isomorphic but the stated bijection is not an isomorphism.
- **M16 / generic 1+3+3+1.** (computed) My grid contained directions such as c ∝ (3,1,1,1) where all three 8s are 1+3+3+1. The skill never says that 1+3+3+1 is the generic pattern for a whole family of c while 4+4 and 1+6+1 need c on special lines. This does not break M17.

## 4. Discriminating consequences
- **E-A. Equivariance test of "circumstances = ordered pairs".**
  - Separates: a rotational-symmetry reading (circumstances as cube edges under the full rotation group) from a person-labelled reading (ordered pairs under S4).
  - Prediction: any Wondrous Wisdom circumstance that behaves as a directed relation (a→c) should be mapped to its complement ordered pair by tetrahedron-swapping symmetry. If the sources treat all 12 circumstances as one undifferentiated orbit under S4, the ordered-pair reading is favoured over the geometric edge reading.
- **E-B. Stabilizer signature.**
  - Separates: faces as unordered pairs (stabilizer V4 or D4-type) from faces as cube faces (stabilizer Z4).
  - Observation: ask whether a conception has a canonical cyclic order on four things (favours Z4) or a symmetric pair and its complement (favours V4). The skill currently asserts both.
- **E-C. Generic versus special grading.**
  - Separates: readings where Father/Son/Spirit are structurally equal under triality from readings where they are distinguished.
  - Observation: for generic c, all three 8s are 1+3+3+1 (e.g. c = (3,1,1,1)); 4+4 and 1+6+1 require c on measure-zero sets. If the theology needs all three patterns, count how many independent symmetry-breakings it names. The mathematics says at least two.
