# Blind brief (phase 1 only)

Read this file and nothing else from this skill before writing your blind
reconstruction. It deliberately omits every structure anyone has proposed.

A body of work describes the mind with three static tables and three
dynamic languages.

- There are 8 *divisions*, 12 *circumstances*, and 6 *conceptions*.
- The author writes: 24 / 2 = 3 × 4 = 12; 24 / 3 = 4 × 2 = 8;
  24 / 4 = 2 × 3 = 6.
- Argumentation takes us from the 12 circumstances to the 6 conceptions.
- Verbalization takes us from the 8 divisions to the 12 circumstances.
- Narration takes us from the 6 conceptions to the 8 divisions.
- Separately, four persons are named: God, I, You, Other. The author
  insists they are not interchangeable.

Propose, with reasons:

1. one or more mathematical structures that carry 8, 12, and 6 objects and
   the three passages between them;
2. how the four persons could be placed in your structure, if at all;
3. what your structure predicts that a mere coincidence of counts would not.

Record your answer before reading anything else. Then continue with phase 2
of `investigator-protocol.md`.
